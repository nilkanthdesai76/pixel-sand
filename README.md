# 🧪 PixelSand: 60 FPS Cellular Automata Physics Sandbox

> A high-performance, locked 60 FPS falling-sand particle physics sandbox built on HTML5 Canvas and typed memory buffers. Features 10 reactive elements with emergent chemical reactions, fluid dynamics, explosive combustion, and gravity manipulation.

---

## ⚡ Features

### 1. 🔬 10 Reactive Chemical Elements
- **Sand**: Granular physical powder that piles up into natural dunes at the angle of repose.
- **Water**: Fluid dynamics with lateral surface dispersion; extinguishes flames, quenches molten lava into stone.
- **Fire**: Rising convection physics; ignites flammable materials and produces smoke/ember trails.
- **Wood**: Rigid organic structure that catches fire and slowly burns down.
- **Gunpowder**: Unstable explosive granules; detonates violently on contact with fire or sparks, blasting shockwaves and debris.
- **Acid**: Corrosive toxic liquid that slowly eats through stone, wood, and sand with sizzle effects.
- **Stone**: Indestructible structural barrier.
- **Oil**: Highly flammable hydrocarbon lighter than water; floats on top of water pools and bursts into raging infernos.
- **Lava**: Molten magma that burns everything in its path and boils water into steam.
- **Plant**: Sprouting vegetation that grows upward when watered and burns rapidly.

### 2. 🎛️ Sandbox & Physics Manipulation
- **Interactive Brush Size**: 1px to 30px brush radius with touch and mouse drag support.
- **Gravity Direction Controls**: Toggle between standard **Earth Gravity (Down)**, **Zero-G (Floating Space)**, and **Inverted Gravity (Up)**.
- **Thermal Heatmap View**: Real-time false-color thermal diffusion overlay.
- **Performance Monitor**: Real-time FPS counter and active particle tally.
- **Snapshot Export**: High-resolution 4x supersampled PNG image export.

```bash
./launch.sh
```
